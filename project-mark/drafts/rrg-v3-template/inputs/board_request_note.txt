Subject: RRG extension - paper for the 22 October meeting

The Relocation and Retention Grant's first term ends on 30 June 2027 and the board has to decide at its 22 October meeting
whether to extend it for 2027 to 2030 or let it lapse. The evaluation agreement in the folder sets out how the board decides;
the Census Bureau's Vintage 2025 county files, the file layouts, the methodology statement, the release notes and the
Census press release for the county estimates are in the folder with it.

Members have seen the press coverage of the March release and several have asked whether Dallas County's growth "collapsed"
last year. The paper should answer the board's question as the agreement frames it.

The analysts who check the board's figures want the workbook, not just the note, so please send both. The program office's
summary of grants paid is in the folder for background only; it does not enter the board's calculation.
