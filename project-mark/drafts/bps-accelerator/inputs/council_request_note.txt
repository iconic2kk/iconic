Capital Area Housing Council | secretariat note | 2026-10-06 | scenario document authored for this exercise

Subject: Accelerator mid-term decision - paper for the 12 November meeting

The council decides on 12 November whether the Bastrop County Housing Accelerator gets a second term (2027 to 2029) or ends
on 31 December 2026. The evaluation agreement signed before launch sets out the test.

The Census Bureau's Building Permits Survey annual files for 2021 to 2024 are in the folder: the county files and the
place-level file for the South region, with the record layouts. Census's survey methodology page and a Census FAQ on the
survey are there too.

The program office's activity summary is in the folder for background; it does not enter the council's calculation.
Members will want the workbook as well as the note.
