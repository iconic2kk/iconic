RSP-STD-4 Summer peak forecast filings

1. Filing

By 31 October each year, every participating area files its forecast of peak-hour demand (MW) for the next summer on the program's filing form. Summer means 1 June to 31 August. Hours are hour-ending UTC, as in EIA-930.

2. Committed forecasts

A forecast may be filed as committed only if it rests on hourly demand covering at least two complete summers for the area as it is configured on the filing date. Committed forecasts are used to set each area's share of the program's reserve obligation.

3. Changes in configuration

Where an area's footprint has changed (balancing authorities formed, retired, merged or split), hourly demand from before the change counts toward section 2 only where the published EIA-930 data report it for exactly the footprint the area has today. History for a predecessor whose footprint differs from today's does not count, and neither do estimates of what today's footprint would have been.

4. Provisional filings

An area that cannot meet section 2 files a provisional forecast instead. A provisional filing reports the highest peak-hour demand observed in a complete summer under the current configuration and states the first 31 October filing date at which the area expects to be able to file a committed forecast. Provisional areas are carried at their observed peak and do not take part in the reserve-share calculation until they file a committed forecast.

5. Data

Filings use EIA-930 demand as EIA publishes it. Where EIA has imputed an hour, the adjusted (imputed) value is used.
