GV-STD-3 Unit verification tiers

1. Purpose

The desk verifies the real-power capability and dynamic model of every synchronous generating unit in the Western footprint on a cycle set by the unit's size. The largest units, which hold most of the footprint's capacity, are verified every year; smaller units less often. Tiers are reset once a year for the following calendar year.

2. Scope

A unit is in scope when it is in service in EIA's latest monthly generator inventory (Form EIA-860M) at the time the tiers are set, it is synchronous, and its plant sits in the Western footprint.

  • In service: status OP (operating) or SB (standby/backup), with net summer capacity above zero.
  • Synchronous: every prime mover except photovoltaic (PV), wind (WT, WS), battery storage (BA) and flywheel (FW).
  • Western footprint: the plant's balancing authority, as the inventory reports it, is in EIA's California, Northwest or Southwest region in the EIA-930 reference tables.

3. Units and size

A unit is a generator, or a group of generators that operates as a single unit; a group counts once. A unit's size is the net summer capacity of its in-scope generators, in MW, as the inventory reports it.

4. Tiers

Rank the in-scope units by size, largest first. Tier 1 is the largest units that together hold at least 50% of in-scope capacity: its boundary is the size of the smallest unit needed to reach 50%, and every unit of at least that size is in Tier 1. Tier 2 is set the same way at 85%, counting Tier 1. Tier 3 is every other in-scope unit. Boundaries are stated in MW as they fall, without rounding.

5. Verification cycle

Tier 1 units are verified every year, Tier 2 units every third year and Tier 3 units every sixth year. A year's test calendar holds every Tier 1 unit, one third of Tier 2 units and one sixth of Tier 3 units, each fraction rounded up to a whole unit.
