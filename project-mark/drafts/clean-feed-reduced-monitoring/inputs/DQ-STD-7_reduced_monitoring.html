DQ-STD-7 Reduced monitoring for clean feeds

1. Purpose

On-call capacity is limited. Each quarter the desk moves the Western balancing authority with the cleanest EIA-930 filing to reduced monitoring for the following quarter: its Sentinel monitors route to the ops channel instead of paging on-call, and its feed is sample-checked once a week.

2. Coverage

Balancing authorities in EIA's California, Northwest and Southwest regions that file demand on Form EIA-930. Generation-only balancing authorities are not covered.

3. Clean hours

A BA's quarter is the hours of its reporting days (the EIA-930 "Data Date") that fall in the calendar quarter.

An hour is clean when every value the BA files for that hour is present as filed and agrees exactly with every other value in the EIA-930 record for the same quantity, and when the BA's demand, net generation and total interchange for the hour balance exactly.

The values a BA files are its demand, day-ahead demand forecast, net generation, net generation by energy source, total interchange, interchange with each directly interconnected balancing authority and, where it reports them, demand by subregion. A value that EIA imputed is not present as filed. The EIA-930 record is EIA's published six-month BALANCE, INTERCHANGE and SUBREGION files.

4. Clean-hour share

A BA's clean-hour share is its clean hours divided by the hours in its quarter, times 100, stated to three decimals.

5. Decision

The covered BA with the highest clean-hour share moves to reduced monitoring for the next quarter. Ties are broken in favour of the BA with the higher average reported demand over the quarter, since moving the larger feed frees more on-call time.
