Subject: Displaced tech worker application - figures for the 4 November meeting

The board's tech jobs tracker (board_tech_jobs_tracker_travis.csv) follows private employment in Travis County in three
industries: data processing and hosting (NAICS 518), other information services (NAICS 519) and computer systems design
(NAICS 5415). The figures are the third-month employment levels from the Bureau of Labor Statistics' Quarterly Census of
Employment and Wages, pulled each quarter by industry code.

Several members point to the tracker's other information services line, which fell from 6,883 jobs at the end of 2021 to
480 at the end of the first quarter of 2022, as the clearest sign of the tech downturn in the county. The draft of the
board's application to the state for displaced-worker funds cites that fall. Before it goes in, the chair wants the cause
of the fall established and the board's figures put on a footing that will survive the state's review.

The QCEW county files for Travis County for 2021 to 2023 (annual and quarterly, all industries and ownerships) are in the
folder with the file layouts and the BLS and Census reference files on industry codes.
