Breadth Fund: projected draws for the 2025 round

Finance office memo to member counties · 30 June 2025 · planning only (charter Article 7)
Scenario document authored for this exercise.

BLS released the 2024 county establishment counts this month, so we can give members an early view of the 2025 round for their grant planning. Each county's projected draw is its 2024 draw moved in line with the change in its count of private manufacturing establishments between 2023 and 2024. The board confirms the actual draws in November.
